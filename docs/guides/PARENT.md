# Parent Guide

## Sign in

Learner portal → **Parent** tab  
`POST /api/v1/auth/parent/login` + tenant slug

## What you can see

- Dashboard: upcoming sessions, homework, alerts  
- Children list (only linked students)  
- Per child: progress, attendance, homework, assessments, tutoring  
- Notifications (in-app)

APIs: `/api/v1/learner/parent/*`

## Fees

When the school issues a student invoice, you may receive a **fee reminder** notification. Pay per your school’s offline/bank process (online checkout may arrive in a later release).

## Privacy

You cannot open another family’s child. If a child is missing, ask the school admin to create a parent–student link.
